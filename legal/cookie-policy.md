## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and store certain information about your preferences or past actions. Docloom also uses similar technologies such as local storage and session tokens, which are covered by this policy alongside traditional cookies.

## 2. How Docloom Uses Cookies

Docloom uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies
These are required for the Service to function and cannot be disabled:
- **Authentication/session cookies:** Maintain your logged-in session after you authenticate via GitHub OAuth.
- **Security cookies:** Support CSRF protection and other security mechanisms that prevent unauthorized requests.

### 2.2 Functional Cookies
These improve your experience but are not strictly required:
- Remembering interface preferences (e.g., dashboard view settings, last-viewed repository).

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies
Used to understand how the Service is used, so we can improve it:
- Basic usage metrics (pages visited, features used, general engagement patterns).
- We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

### 2.4 Third-Party Cookies
Some third-party services we rely on may set their own cookies when their components load as part of the Service:
- **Cloudflare:** may set cookies related to security and performance (e.g., bot mitigation, load balancing).
- **Dodo Payments:** may set cookies during the hosted checkout flow to support payment processing and fraud prevention.

We do not control these third-party cookies directly; their use is governed by the respective third party's own cookie and privacy policies.

## 3. Cookies We Do Not Use

Docloom does not use cookies for behavioral advertising, does not sell cookie-derived data to advertisers or data brokers, and does not use cookies to build cross-site advertising profiles of you.

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- View what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
- Block third-party cookies.
- Block all cookies from specific sites or all sites.
- Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies (such as authentication cookies) will prevent you from logging in or using core features of the Service.

If we introduce non-essential cookies subject to consent requirements in your jurisdiction (e.g., EU/UK), we will provide a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or decline those categories before they are set.
